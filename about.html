About Vijaya Trust

We exist to uplift underprivileged women and children through education, skill development, and community care.

🌸

Our Mission

To create a dignified, self-reliant life for every woman and child we serve — regardless of where they were born or how much their family earns.

🌱

Our Vision

A society where gender and poverty are never barriers to human potential.

What We Believe

Our Core Values

🛡️

Dignity

📊

Community

How We Started

Our Story

Vijaya Trust was born from a simple observation: thousands of women in Bengaluru's underserved neighbourhoods had the capability to transform their lives — they just needed access, support, and someone to believe in them.

We started with a single skill-training workshop in 2026. Today, we run programs across education, nutrition, health, and legal awareness — all powered by a growing team of passionate volunteers.

The name Vijaya means victory — the victory of every woman over hardship, and every child over the cycle of poverty. That is the victory we fight for every single day.

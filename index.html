Who We Are

A Trust Built on Belief

Founded in 2026 in Bengaluru, Vijaya Trust was born from a simple conviction — every woman and child deserves a fair chance, regardless of where they were born.

The name Vijaya means victory — the victory we fight for every day.

Our Full Story

What We Do

Our Programs

Two focused pillars — one for the women who carry families, one for the children who carry the future.

✂️

Skill Training

Tailoring, embroidery, and computer literacy for sustainable livelihoods.

📚

Education Support

School fees, stationery, and tutoring for children in need.

💊

Health Awareness

Maternal health, nutrition workshops, and free medical camps.

🍱

Nutrition Program

Daily mid-day meals and monthly nutrition packages for children.

⚖️

Legal Rights

Know-your-rights sessions and government welfare guidance.

🏅

Scholarships

Annual merit-cum-need scholarships for bright students.

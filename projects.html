What We Do

Our Programs

Two focused pillars — one for the women who carry families, one for the children who carry the future.

For Women

👩 Women Empowerment

Practical programs that help women build skills, confidence, and independent livelihoods.

✂️ Skills · Livelihood

Skill Training Workshop

Train underprivileged women in tailoring, embroidery, handicrafts, and basic computer literacy to help them build sustainable, independent livelihoods.

Active
💊 Health · Nutrition

Health Awareness Camp

Monthly health camps covering maternal health, nutrition, hygiene, and access to government health schemes. Conducted in partnership with local clinics.

Active
⚖️ Legal · Rights

Legal Rights Awareness

Know-your-rights sessions helping women understand domestic violence laws, property rights, and how to access government welfare programs.

Active
🛡️ Safety · Confidence

Self-Defence Classes

Basic self-defence training to build personal safety, confidence, and awareness among women in underserved communities.

Active
For Children

👧 Child Welfare

Programs that give every child a chance at education, nutrition, and a brighter future.

📚 Education · Scholarships

Education Support Program

School fee assistance, stationery kits, uniforms, and one-on-one tutoring for children from low-income families to keep them in school.

Active
🍱 Nutrition · Health

Nutrition Program

Daily nutritious mid-day meals and monthly grocery packages for malnourished children across partner communities.

Active
🎨 Arts · Sports

After-School Activities

Art, music, sports, and creative workshops run every weekday afternoon to give children a safe, stimulating space outside school hours.

Active
🏅 Scholarship · Education

Annual Scholarship

Merit-cum-need based annual scholarships for bright students from families below the poverty line, covering tuition and educational materials.

Active
